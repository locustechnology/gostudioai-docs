## Two ways to use it

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Create">
    Describe what you want and get a new image. For example, "a red fox in the snow at sunrise".
  </Card>
  <Card title="Edit">
    Send one or more photos and say what to change. For example, "change the background to a beach at sunset".
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to use it
1. **Choose create or edit.** Create starts from words only. Edit starts from your photos.
2. **Write your description.** Say what you want to see: the subject, the place, the light and the style.
3. **Pick a model** (`model_id`), or leave it to the default (see below).
4. **Choose the quality or size.** Higher settings give more detail and cost more.
5. **Choose a shape** (`aspect_ratio`), such as square, portrait or wide, or let the model decide with `auto`. When editing, `auto` keeps the shape of your photo.

## Which model to use

A model offers either quality levels or sizes, never both. The full, current list is always in [Image models](/api-reference/ai-image/image-models).

| Model (`model_id`) | Options | Photos to edit |
|---|---|---|
| `gpt-image-2-6-sunburst` (default) | Quality: low, medium, high, xhigh, max | Up to 10 |
| `gpt-image-2-6-flare` | Quality: low, medium, high, xhigh, max | Up to 10 |
| `nano-banana-2.1` | Size: 1K, 2K, 4K | Up to 10 |
| `gpt-image-2` | Quality: low, medium, high | Up to 10 |
| `nano-banana-pro` | Size: 1K, 2K, 4K | Up to 10 |
| `pruna` | One fixed size | 1 |
| `minimax-h3-image` | One fixed size | Up to 10 |

Not sure? Start with the default.

<Note>
AI Image can edit photos of people, but the face may change slightly. When the face must stay exactly the same person, use [Recreate](/guides/recreate).
</Note>

## Tips
- When editing, name only what should change. Everything else stays the same.
- See [Pricing](/resources/pricing) for the cost of each model and setting.
