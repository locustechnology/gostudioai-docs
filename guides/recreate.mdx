## Why Recreate?
AI Image can edit and create photos of people too. But when the face must look exactly like the real person, use **Recreate**. It works from a trained profile of that person, so the face stays accurate in every photo, not just similar.

| You want to | Use |
|---|---|
| Change a background, outfit or detail in a photo | [AI Image](/guides/ai-image) |
| New photos where the face must be exactly the person | **Recreate** |

## What is a profile?
A **profile** is a person trained from their photos. Every image made with it looks like that person, not a generic AI face. Train once, then use it as often as you like.

## How to use it

1. **Train a profile** in your GoStudio account. Upload 10 to 30 photos of the person: different angles, expressions and settings, with the face clearly visible.
2. **Wait until it is ready.** Ready profiles appear in your profile list, and in [List profiles](/api-reference/profiles/list-profiles).
3. **Choose what to make** with it (below).
4. **Get the result.** These photos are made in the background and take a little longer than a simple edit.

## Which model to use

Profile Photo and Recreate Group offer two models (`model_id`):

| Model | Best for |
|---|---|
| `nano-banana-2` (default) | The standard choice. Costs less than `gpt-image-2` at 4K. |
| `gpt-image-2` | A second model with its own look. Same price as `nano-banana-2` at 1K and 2K. |

Both come in 1K, 2K or 4K. You can ask for up to 4 photos at once.

## Tips
- The better the training photos, the better every result. Avoid sunglasses, heavy filters and group shots.
- For Recreate Group, the order of profiles matches the people in the scene from the first person onward.
