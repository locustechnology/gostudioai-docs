## Apps

**Age Modifier**
- "See yourself older or younger" features for social and entertainment apps.
- Aging previews for family, memory and gift apps.
- Before-and-after content for social media.

**Watermark Remover**
- Cleaning product photos for store and marketplace listings.
- Removing logos, text or date stamps from your own images.
- Preparing clean images for reuse in marketing.

**Weight Shift**
- Goal previews in fitness, diet and wellness apps.
- Motivation and progress features for coaches and gyms.

**Group Photo**
- Family photos when not everyone could be there.
- Team and company photos for websites and LinkedIn.
- Friends, wedding and event photos made from individual selfies.

## AI Image

**Create**
- Social media posts and ad creatives.
- Blog, website and presentation visuals.
- Product scenes and lifestyle shots without a photoshoot.
- Concept art, mood boards and design ideas.

**Edit**
- New backgrounds or settings for a photo.
- Changing outfits, colors or small details.
- Combining several images into one scene.
- Ad variations from a single product photo.

## AI Video

- **From a description:** short social clips, ads and story scenes from an idea.
- **From a photo:** bring a portrait, product shot or artwork to life. Use a start and an end photo to control how the video moves.
- **From references:** videos that feature a specific person, product or character, staying consistent from shot to shot.
- **Edit a video:** restyle footage, change the setting or add effects to a clip you already have.

## Recreate

The most accurate way to keep a real person's face.


- **Professional headshots** for LinkedIn, resumes, websites and company pages.
- **Personal branding** photos for creators, coaches and consultants.
- **Recreate a look:** match the style of a photo you love, with your own face.
- **Themed photos:** seasonal, travel, holiday and event photos of the same person.
- **Group scenes** with several real people who were never photographed together.
