## Sending your photos and videos
You send each photo or video as a **link**, not as a file.

- The link must be **public**: anyone with it can open the file without logging in.
- It must start with **https://**.
- Keep the file online until your result is ready.
- Cloud storage links (such as a CDN or a storage bucket) work well. Links to web pages, or links that need a login, do not.

## File formats

| Type | Use | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Photos | **JPEG** or **PNG** work everywhere. WebP is also fine. | Keep files reasonably sized; very large photos are slower |
| Videos (for video edit) | **MP4**, **MOV** or **WebM** | Up to 100 MB |

For video edit, the clip must fit the limits of the model you choose. They are listed under each model in [Video models](/api-reference/ai-video/video-models).

## Start with a good photo
The result can only be as good as the photo you start with.

- Use a sharp, well-lit photo where the face is clearly visible.
- Use the largest, sharpest version of the photo you have.
- Show one person per photo, unless the tool is made for groups.
- Avoid heavy filters, sunglasses and hands covering the face.

## Write clear descriptions
- **Describe what you want, not what you don't.** "A sunny beach" works better than "not indoors".
- **Be specific.** Mention the place, the light and the style, for example "a rooftop at sunset, soft warm light, editorial style".
- **When editing, name only the change.** Everything you don't mention stays as it is.

## If a result is blocked
Our safety filters block some photos and descriptions. If that happens, try a different photo or wording. You are not charged for blocked results.
