Apps are the quickest way to add a feature. Each one does a single job and picks the right model for you. You only send a photo and one or two choices.

## Age Modifier
Shows how a person would look at a different age, from a child to 100.

**How to use it**
1. Send a clear photo of one person.
2. Choose the age to show (`target_age`).
3. Optionally, choose whether to show just that age or a progression over time (`age_mode`), and the person's `gender`.

**Tip:** a front-facing photo with the face clearly visible gives the most natural result.

## Watermark Remover
Removes watermarks, logos, text and stamps from a photo.

**How to use it**
1. Send the photo.
2. Choose a quality level (`model_id`):
   - **`lite`:** one click, instant results, no description needed.
   - **`pro`:** fast results, and you can describe what to remove.
   - **`advance`:** guided by your description, with a sharper, upscaled result.
3. With `pro` or `advance`, you can say what to remove in `instructions`, for example "the logo in the bottom corner".

**Tip:** only remove marks from images you own or have the right to use.

## Weight Shift
Shows the person in a photo leaner or fuller.

**How to use it**
1. Send a photo of one person, ideally showing most of the body.
2. Choose how much to change (`weight_level`): three steps leaner or three steps fuller.

## Group Photo
Puts 2 to 20 people into one photo, in the scene of a photo you choose.

**How to use it**
1. Send one photo of each person.
2. Send the scene to recreate, such as a group photo you like.
3. Optionally, add wishes like "everyone smiling".
4. Choose `low`, `medium` or `high` resolution.

**Tips**
- Use one clear photo per person, with the face fully visible.
- The number of people does not change the price, only the resolution does.
- Group photos take longer, so they are made in the background.
