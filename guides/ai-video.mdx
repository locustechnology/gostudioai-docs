## Four ways to make a video

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="From a description">
    Say what should happen. Good for social clips and ads from an idea.
  </Card>
  <Card title="From a photo">
    Start from a photo, and with some models an ending photo too. Good for bringing a still image to life.
  </Card>
  <Card title="From references">
    Show the people, products or characters to feature. They stay consistent through the video.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Edit a video">
    Send a short clip and say what to change. Good for restyling footage you already have.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to use it
1. **Choose the way** that matches what you have: an idea, a photo, reference images or a clip.
2. **Describe the video:** what happens, the camera movement, the mood.
3. **Pick a model** (`model_id`), or leave it to the default.
4. **Choose the length and resolution.** Longer and sharper videos cost more.
5. **Turn sound on** (`audio`), if the model can add it.
6. **Wait for the result.** Videos take longer than images, so they are made in the background.

## Tips
- See [Pricing](/resources/pricing) for the cost per second of each model.
- Longer clips and higher resolutions cost more, so test with a short, low-resolution clip first.
