## Welcome to the GoStudio API

GoStudio is an AI visual studio that keeps identity intact: your face or your product, across professional photos, product photography and video. Curated models, no learning curve, professional results in minutes.

Generic AI image tools struggle to keep a real person looking like themselves. GoStudio is built around that problem. Train a person once, and every photo and video made with them still looks like them. No generic AI faces, no identity drift.

**Base URL:** `https://www.gostudio.ai/api/v2`

## What the API offers

The GoStudio API brings these tools into your own app or website.

| API | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Apps](/guides/apps) | Ready-made tools: Age Modifier, Watermark Remover, Weight Shift and Group Photo |
| [AI Image](/guides/ai-image) | Create an image from a description, or edit a photo |
| [AI Video](/guides/ai-video) | Make a video from a description, a photo, reference images or a clip |
| [Recreate](/guides/recreate) | New photos of a trained person, alone or in a group |
| [Account](/api-reference/my-data/get-credit-balance) | Your credits, generations, images and uploads |

You send a photo or a description. You get back a finished image or video, ready to use. You pay in credits only for what you make, and credits are returned if a job fails.
