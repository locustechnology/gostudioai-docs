Email **[support@gostudio.ai](mailto:support@gostudio.ai)** and we will get back to you.

## Include these details

The more you tell us, the faster we can help.

- The **`job_id`**, if the problem is about a specific job.
- The **endpoint** you called and the time of the request.
- The **error** you received: `type`, `code` and `message`.
- What you expected to happen.

<Warning>
Never send your API key. If you think your key has been exposed, generate a new one in **Account settings**. See [Authentication](/api-reference/authentication).
</Warning>

## Before you write

- Look up the error `code` on [Errors](/resources/errors).
- Check what a request costs on [Pricing](/resources/pricing).
- For jobs that are still running, follow [Asynchronous requests](/api-reference/async-requests) and poll `poll_url`.
